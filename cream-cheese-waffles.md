Ingredients
===========

375 g flour
100 g sugar
475 g milk
1 teaspoon baking powder
2 eggs
10 ml vanilla extract
1 teaspoon nutmeg
2 teaspoons cinnamon
110 g cream cheese

Instructions
============

Mix ingredients, bake in waffle iron.
