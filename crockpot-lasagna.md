Ingredients
===========

- 1 bunch chopped fresh basil
- 425 g ricotta cheese
- 2 large eggs
- 1 teaspoon garlic powder
- 1 teaspoon kosher salt
- ½ teaspoon freshly cracked black pepper
- 700 g marinara sauce
- 450 g lasagna noodles
- 225 g mushrooms, sliced
- 2 small zucchini, sliced
- 2 small yellow squash, sliced
- 150 g frozen spinach
- 450 g low-moisture mozzarella, grated
- 225 g fresh mozzarella, thinly sliced
- Freshly grated Parmesan, to taste 

Method
======

- In a medium bowl, combine the basil, ricotta, eggs, garlic powder, salt and pepper.
- In the bottom of a large pan with lid, spread ½ dl of marinara and top with a single layer of uncooked lasagna noodles. Dollop with ⅓ of the ricotta mixture and spread into a thin layer.
- Layer with ⅓ each of the mushroom, zucchini, and yellow squash. Top with ⅓ of the chopped spinach, followed by ⅓ of the grated mozzarella. Lastly, layer with ¼ of the remaining sauce. Repeat the layers 2 more times. Finish with a layer of uncooked lasagna noodles and remaining sauce. 
- Bake in the oven at 150 degrees for about 1½ hour.
- Top with the fresh mozzarella and bake for 10-15 minutes.
- Garnish with Parmesan cheese and basil.
